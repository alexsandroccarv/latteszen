About lattesZen

A Lattes Curriculum organizer that runs entirely in your browser — no server, no account, and your data is never sent anywhere.

What it is

lattesZen helps researchers, educators and public-service professionals catalog, document and publish their Lattes Curriculum output, keeping the data in your browser and in a folder you choose on your own computer (or, optionally, in your Google Drive) — never on a third-party server.

It's a free-software project (AGPLv3 license), with open-source code on GitHub, maintained independently by Alexsandro Cardoso Carvalho.

Main features

  • Catalog items across every Lattes category (Education, Professional Activities, Projects, Output, Patents/Registrations, Innovation, Science-and-Technology Education and Outreach, Events, Advising, Committees), with maximum field correspondence to the Lattes Platform.
  • Import the Lattes XML in the official format, with deduplication — re-importing doesn't create duplicate items.
  • Attach evidence (PDFs, images) to each item, saved locally by category.
  • Compliance: a dashboard showing what's already documented, what evidence is missing, and what required fields are missing.
  • Publish Online: generates a public HTML page of the curriculum, ready to host.
  • RSC-PCCTAE module (optional, for Brazilian federal technical-administrative staff): points and level simulation from the cataloged items.

Privacy by default

No server of our own, no sign-up, no login. Everything you enter stays in your browser and, by default, in a folder on your computer — nothing is sent to us.

Full details in the Privacy Policy and Terms of Use.

Open source and citation
