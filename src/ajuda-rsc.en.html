RSC-PCCTAE in lattesZen

How to use the Recognition of Knowledge and Skills (RSC) module to simulate points and the achievable level, and generate the memorial. The general curriculum flow (folder, import, fields, compliance) is covered in the Lattes help.

Important notice: this module is an individual-use support tool, based on the tables of Decree No. 13,048/2026 (a Brazilian federal regulation, RSC-PCCTAE has no equivalent outside Brazil). The calculations are estimates for personal organization and have no official standing — the formal evaluation is the responsibility of your institution's committee. Always check the rules in force and the original annexes.

What RSC-PCCTAE is

RSC (Recognition of Knowledge and Skills) lets a technical-administrative staff member in education (PCCTAE, a Brazilian federal civil-service career track) obtain, for the purposes of a Qualification Incentive, a recognition equivalent to an education level higher than the one they hold, based on activities, output and responsibilities performed.

In practice, you gather evidence distributed across six requirements (Annexes I–VI of the decree); each activity is worth a number of points, and the total — combined with the number of distinct criteria met and your education level — determines the achievable RSC level (RSC-I, RSC-II, RSC-III).

lattesZen reuses the items you already catalog for Lattes: instead of re-entering everything, you just mark which items count toward RSC and which criterion they belong to.

1 Enable the module

RSC is off by default. To turn it on:

  1. Open the Settings tab.
  2. In the RSC-PCCTAE (optional) section, check Enable RSC-PCCTAE module.
  3. Fill in your staff data: position, class/level, SIAPE ID (optional, the Brazilian federal civil-service registry number), department, hire date and start of the counting period.
  4. Choose your education level — it caps the maximum RSC level you can reach and sets the corresponding Qualification Incentive (IQ).
  5. Click Save RSC.
Once enabled, a new RSC tab appears (the simulator) and, inside the form for each eligible item, an RSC layer appears just below the regular fields.

2 Mark items for RSC (RSC layer)

With the module on, edit an item on the Catalog tab. Just below the fields, on the "Publish" row:

  1. Check "use for RSC" (next to "Lattes" and "Web") — the RSC layer fields appear next.
  2. Choose the matching criterion — the list is organized by the six requirements (Annexes I–VI); each option shows the description, the unit of measure and the points.
  3. When the criterion asks for it, provide the role/subtype (e.g., coordination vs. participation).
  4. Write a justification — it goes into the memorial.
  5. The app calculates and shows the item's points right away (quantity × unit value, according to the unit: per year/fraction, per month, or per unit).

Items enabled for RSC get an amber marker in the Compliance tab's list, showing the criterion and the points.

Calculation units: year (per year or fraction over 6 months, uses the start/end dates), month (per month), and unit (counts 1 per appointment/project/product/award/event).

3 Items with no Lattes counterpart — categories 20 and 21

Some RSC criteria have no matching element in the Lattes Curriculum. For these cases there are dedicated categories, not tied to Lattes, which only appear in the catalog when the RSC module is enabled:

  • 12 — Research Groups: leadership/deputy leadership or membership in a registered research/outreach group (Lattes has nothing for this — the official registry is CNPq's Directory of Research Groups).
  • 21 — Public Health Crisis Response: institutional action responding to an outbreak, epidemic or pandemic.

Use these to record such activities with a justification and evidence. They count toward the RSC simulator, but are not exported in the Lattes XML. For other administrative activities with no dedicated Lattes element (contract management, procurement, management position/gratified function, department responsibility, etc.), use the existing free-text fields under Activities — see the full criterion-by-criterion mapping in Relating Lattes and RSC.

4 The RSC tab (simulator)

The RSC tab consolidates everything you've marked and shows:

  • Achievable level (RSC-I / II / III) highlighted, respecting the cap set by your education level.
  • Total points and number of distinct criteria met.
  • The list of counted items, grouped by Requirement (Annexes I–VI) — each group already shows items, criteria and the point sum right in its header.

This lets you see where to focus effort — for example, if you just need to diversify criteria instead of stacking more points on the same requirement.

Only items that are marked to count, have a criterion chosen, and have not already been used in a previous recognition enter the calculation.

5 Memorial and spreadsheet

Still on the RSC tab, you can generate supporting documents to put together the process:

  • Generate memorial — a text file (rsc-memorial.txt) with your data, the items by requirement, the justifications and the score.
  • Spreadsheet (CSV) — a table with requirement, criterion, description, item title, period, role, quantity, unit value, points and evidence, for review and attachment to the process.
  • Save form (.docx) — generates the standard application form from the Annex of MEC Ordinance No. 608/2026 (staff identification, requested level, activities by requirement and compliance statement), already as an editable .docx document for Word/LibreOffice. Unlike the other three, it isn't downloaded through the browser — it's saved directly into the Exportação/RSC-PCCTAE folder of your configured directory (which is why it requires a storage directory set up in Settings).
  • Generate prompt (AI) — a .md file (prompt-trajetoria-profissional.md) ready to paste into an AI (Claude, ChatGPT, etc.) to generate the Professional Trajectory section of the Descriptive Memorial, required by Art. 13 of Decree No. 13,048/2026. It brings a master prompt with the official rules (objective text between 4,000 and 10,000 characters, first person, no sensitive personal data) followed by your categorized data (position, department, education level and the activities by requirement, with criterion, period and justification) — deliberately without name, SIAPE ID, phone/email or process numbers, to minimize what's sent to an external tool.
Since each item can have its evidence (PDF) attached through the normal curriculum flow, the memorial, the spreadsheet, the form and the prompt already reflect the proof you've organized.

The six requirements (Annexes I–VI)

Req.Topic
IGroups, commissions, committees and institutional representation
IIInstitutional projects and support for teaching, research and outreach
IIIAwards and public recognition
IVTechnical-administrative or specialized responsibilities
VManagement/advisory position or role
VIProduction, prospecting and dissemination of knowledge

The point values for each criterion, the minimums per level and the cap by education level are built into the app per the decree. Since the rule can be amended by a supplementary act, always check the version in force before formalizing your process.
