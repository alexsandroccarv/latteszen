What is lattesZen

lattesZen is a Lattes Curriculum organizer that runs entirely in your browser — no data ever leaves your computer. It helps you keep your CV items organized, backed by evidence (with an attached PDF), and ready for publication, and it also simulates RSC-PCCTAE (Recognition of Knowledge and Skills) for administrative-technical staff in education, a Brazilian career-progression program.

First steps (5 minutes)

  1. Choose a folder under Settings → Storage directory (or connect Google Drive, if you'd rather access it from more than one device).
  2. Bring in your items: import the XML exported from the Lattes Platform, or look up your works by ORCID iD, or import a BibTeX/RIS file — all under Settings.
  3. Attach supporting evidence (PDFs) to each item under the Catalog tab, using the Inbox or dragging the file straight onto the form.
  4. Check what's missing under the Compliance tab — evidence and required fields, all in one panel.
  5. Publish whenever you're ready, under Publish on the Web: it generates a self-contained public CV page from the same data.
Browser: automatic saving to a folder uses the File System Access API, available in Google Chrome and Microsoft Edge (desktop). In other browsers, or using Google Drive, the app works normally.

Quick questions

Where is my data saved?

In the folder you choose on your own computer (or, optionally, a dedicated folder in your Google Drive). lattesZen has no server of its own and never sends your data outside your browser.

Do I need to re-type everything I already have on Lattes?

No — import the XML exported from the Lattes Platform (or your works via ORCID/BibTeX/RIS) and the app brings in the items automatically, without duplicating them on future imports.

Does the RSC simulator have any official standing?

No — it's a support tool for individual use, based on the tables from the decree currently in force, to help you organize your score and supporting evidence. The formal evaluation is always up to your institution's committee.
