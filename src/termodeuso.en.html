2. What lattesZen is

lattesZen is a free, open-source tool that runs entirely in your browser, to help you organize your Lattes Curriculum: entering items with evidence, checking compliance, assembling the RSC-PCCTAE dossier, and publishing a curriculum page online.

lattesZen is not the Lattes Platform, is not maintained by CNPq or any Brazilian Federal Government body, and does not replace official channels — it's an independently-run support tool.
