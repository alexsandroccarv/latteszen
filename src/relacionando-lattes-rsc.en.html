Relating Lattes and RSC

Reference table cross-referencing each of the 55 specific criteria from the six Annexes of the RSC-PCCTAE Decree (a Brazilian federal regulation) with the corresponding element/attribute in the official Lattes Curriculum schema (CurriculoLattes.xsd) — useful to understand why some criteria don't have a "natural" place in Lattes, and what lattesZen already covers with a category of its own.

How to read this table: the Lattes Curriculum correspondence column describes the closest XSD element/attribute to the criterion — in many cases it's an approximation (free text), not an exact match. Where lattesZen has already solved the gap with a category of its own (outside Lattes, RSC-only), a lattesZen: … label appears next to the criterion.
